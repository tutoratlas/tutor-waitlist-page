Draft waitlist terms.

Last updated 16 September 2026 · Draft pending review by the captain and jiehao

These short terms cover the Tutor Atlas waitlist only. There is no product to buy here and no account to open yet.

1. Joining the waitlist

Joining the waitlist means you give us your name and Telegram handle so we can contact you about early access. It does not create a paid account, a service contract, or a duty for you to use Tutor Atlas later.

Please use a Telegram handle that belongs to you. You can ask to leave the waitlist at any time by emailing hello@tutoratlas.sg.

2. Early access is not guaranteed

Being on the waitlist is not a reservation and not a place in a fixed queue. We may invite some tutors before others, delay early access, change the early-access plan, or decide not to offer access.

3. This site is informational

The site explains what Tutor Atlas is being built to do. It is not a promise that every described feature is available today or will launch in the same form.

4. Privacy

The waitlist privacy notice explains what the waitlist collects, why we collect it, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how to ask for deletion.

5. Changes before launch

These terms may change before Tutor Atlas launches. If a fuller product becomes available, it should have fuller product terms before you use it.
