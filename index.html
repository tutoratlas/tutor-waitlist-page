FAQ

Straight answers before you join

Is Tutor Atlas another tutoring marketplace?

No. Tutor Atlas is a personalised AI agent for tutors who already have students. It is not a parent marketplace and it does not redistribute your book.

What does the AI agent actually do?

You get a personalised PA for your book of record: lessons logged while they are still fresh, schedule and payment context held for you, and paste-ready bill text for WhatsApp when month-end arrives — so you keep time for teaching instead of rebuilding a spreadsheet. The same agent is built to become your trusted record across public agency Telegram channels — watching for fits, helping you apply with your confirmation, and tracking what you have already tried. Daily lesson admin comes first.

Does the AI agent handle tuition assignments?

Yes in the product direction — same personalised agent as your lesson admin, not a second product. It is built to listen across public agency Telegram channels the instant fits post, format your details to each agency’s requirements, prepare an apply you still confirm, and remember attempts across channels that otherwise keep separate records. Lesson admin (students, schedule, payments) is the daily-driver first; assignment handling attaches later. It is not a parent marketplace and it does not apply without your say-so.

Will Tutor Atlas poach my students or share my book?

No. Your student book stays yours. Privacy and trust come first. We are not here to advertise your relationships.

Do parents need an account?

Not in the first release. You work with the agent; parents receive what you already send today — for example paste-ready bill text you copy into WhatsApp. The agent does not message parents for you.

Who is Tutor Atlas for?

Independent home tutors in Singapore who run their own students and want evenings back from admin — a clear book for lessons, schedule and payments, paste-ready bills for WhatsApp, and (later on the same agent) assignment-channel memory when you have capacity. If you mainly want a parent marketplace, this waitlist is not the right door.

Is pricing listed here?

No. Public pricing is not on this page. Join the waitlist if fuss-free admin on standby fits how you already teach. We will share commercial details when early access is relevant.

When will it launch?

We are collecting high intent tutors first. Timing depends on build readiness with our technical partner. Waitlist members hear first.
